### Key Takeaways & Reflection Questions

We had an incredible amount of engagement during the live session! To keep that momentum going, here are a few core questions from our recent discussions to think about as you review the material:


- Epistemic Power & Control: When Indigenous ecological knowledge enters corporate, state, or scientific technology frameworks, does it truly democratize problem-solving? Or does it relocate power to the institutions capable of coding, financing, and scaling it?


- Problem Definition: Who gets to decide which climate problems AI is designed to solve, and how do we ensure these models respect local context rather than treating culture as raw data?


- Data Sovereignty: How can we ensure Indigenous communities maintain full ownership, authority, and veto power over how their sacred knowledge is digitally stored and used (such as through the CARE Principles)?
