### Key takeaways from today’s lecture:





- AI complements remote sensing by enabling automated analysis of large-scale satellite data.



- Time-series analysis is essential for detecting deforestation, forest degradation, wildfire impacts, and vegetation recovery.



- Different AI approaches—from Random Forests to foundation models—each have strengths depending on the application.



- Reliable forest monitoring requires combining satellite imagery with field observations, LiDAR, climate, and other data sources.



- The future lies in explainable, trustworthy AI that supports evidence-based forest management and conservation.
