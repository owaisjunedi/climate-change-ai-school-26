### Key takeaways from today's lecture:

●      Climate finance connects three concepts of (Historical) Responsibility, Ability (to pay), and Vulnerability.

●      International climate finance aims to provide financial resources from countries with greater responsibility and capability to support countries with greater vulnerability.

●      AI can integrate scattered and unstructured data to form concrete evidence to inform financial policy and investment decisions.

●      AI is useful for analyzing climate-related financial risks, including transition risks and physical risks.

●      Always validate extracted information and predictions against other datasets, expert hypotheses, and real-world outcomes.
